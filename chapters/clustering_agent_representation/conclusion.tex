\label{sec:clustering_agent_representation_conclusion}

In this chapter, we introduced \emphasize{\methodName} (\gls{car}), a method that extracts behaviorally meaningful clusters from an agent's \gls{ls} to enhance explainability.

In parallel, advancing automated cluster interpretation and developing human-centered evaluation protocols would strengthen both the explainability and the practical relevance of the approach.

\section{Manuscript Structure}
\label{sec:manuscript_structure}

The chapters of this manuscript fall into three categories, distinguished throughout by color (Figure~\ref{fig:introduction_manuscript_map}):

\begin{itemize}
    \item[]\textcolor{myblue}{Technical background} chapters recall how a field already established in the literature works, without claiming any original contribution: \gls{dl} (Chapter~\ref{chap:deep_learning}) and \gls{rl} (Chapter~\ref{chap:reinforcement_learning}).
    \item[]\textcolor{mygreen}{Survey} chapters review the state of the art on a specific question.
    Their contribution lies not only in the content they cover but, just as much, in how it is organized.
    To our knowledge, the way each of these chapters structures its literature is unique.
    This applies to our review of latent-space analysis methods (Chapter~\ref{chap:latent_space}) and to our taxonomy of \gls{xrl} techniques (Chapter~\ref{chap:explainability_reinforcement_learning}).
    \item[]\textcolor{myred}{Method} chapters present our contribution to \gls{xrl}. They are split in two parts: the description of \gls{car} (Chapter~\ref{chap:clustering_agent_representation}) and its experimental evaluation (Chapter~\ref{chap:experiments}).
\end{itemize}

This manuscript is, first and foremost, the account of a body of research, written for the reviewers and the jury who will examine it.
It is nonetheless written to be as accessible as possible.
The background chapters restate the concepts they rely on, so that any reader familiar with \gls{dl} or \gls{rl} can follow the manuscript without consulting external material.
Readers already familiar with a given field may skip its background chapter directly: Chapter~\ref{chap:deep_learning} for \gls{dl}, Chapter~\ref{chap:reinforcement_learning} for \gls{rl}.
Readers primarily interested in explainability can move directly to Chapters~\ref{chap:latent_space} and~\ref{chap:explainability_reinforcement_learning}, and readers interested only in our method can follow the minimal path Chapter~\ref{chap:latent_space}~$\rightarrow$~Chapter~\ref{chap:clustering_agent_representation}~$\rightarrow$~Chapter~\ref{chap:experiments}, referring back to earlier chapters as needed.
Notation and acronyms used throughout the manuscript are collected in the glossary at the end of the document.

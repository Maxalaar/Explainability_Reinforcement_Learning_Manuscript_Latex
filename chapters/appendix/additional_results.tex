\chapter{Additional Results}
\label{chap:appendix}

This appendix reports, for each of the six Atari environments studied in Chapter~\ref{chap:experiments}, the full evolution of \gls{cu}, \gls{as}, and \gls{acu} with respect to the number of clusters $K \in [2,20]$, for every analyzed layer, underlying the analysis of Section~\ref{sec:experiments_latent_space_analysis}. All three metrics are computed with the adjusted Rand index, consistent with the rest of this manuscript.

For each environment, one table is reported per metric, with the analyzed layers as rows and $K \in [2,20]$ as columns. In each table, a value in \textbf{bold} is the highest value of its column, i.e., the best layer for that number of clusters; a value that is \underline{underlined} is the highest value of its row, i.e., the best number of clusters for that layer; a value both \textbf{\underline{bold and underlined}} is simultaneously the best of its row and of its column; and a value in \textcolor{red}{red} is the single highest value of the whole table, all rows and columns combined. The last column and the last row report, respectively, the mean $\pm$ standard deviation across $K$ for each layer, and the mean $\pm$ standard deviation across layers for each $K$; the bottom-right cell reports the mean $\pm$ standard deviation over the whole table.

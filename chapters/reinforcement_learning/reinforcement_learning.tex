\chaptercontents

\glsresetall

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of \gls{rl}, providing the necessary foundation for the ideas developed throughout the rest of this thesis.
It first traces the milestones that led \gls{rl} to emerge as a unified field.
It then clarifies what is meant by an agent and how it relates to the \gls{rl} algorithm, before formalizing the problem that such algorithms aim to solve.
Next, it explains how policies are progressively refined through interaction with the environment to approach the optimal policy.
Finally, it closes with a summary of the key ideas and a discussion of the broader significance of \gls{rl}.

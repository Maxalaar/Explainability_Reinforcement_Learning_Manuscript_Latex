\subsection{\SurroundName}

Two clusterings are examined for \SurroundName (Table~\ref{tab:qualitative_surround_metrics}), obtained at layer 8b with $K=4$ and at layer 7b with $K=6$.
Both partitions combine three kinds of concepts: the start of the episode (clusters B and J), its outcome, when the opponent loses (clusters D and F), and the region of the level in which the player moves.

At layer 8b (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_surround}), this region is described coarsely: the trail of the player is either confined to the bottom-left area (cluster A) or runs along the top of the level after arriving from the left (cluster C).
At layer 7b (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_surround_alt}), it is refined into lower-level concepts: the player moves in the upper-left corner (cluster E) or in the lower-left corner (cluster G), the end of the game is played in the lower-left corner (cluster H), and the player crosses the level from the top left to the bottom right (cluster I).
Both clusterings reach the same \gls{cu}, \gls{as}, and \gls{acu} ($0.67$, $0.71$, and $0.38$): the refinement neither improves nor degrades the metrics, although the variability across surrogate policies is larger with $K=6$ (standard deviation of $0.04$ against $0.02$).

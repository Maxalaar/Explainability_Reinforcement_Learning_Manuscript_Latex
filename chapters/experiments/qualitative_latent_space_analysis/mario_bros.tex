\subsection{\MarioBrosName}

Two clusterings are examined for \MarioBrosName, obtained at layer 3 with $K=3$ and at layer 8b with $K=4$.
At the shallower layer, rather than separating phases of the episode, the clustering separates the side of the screen on which Mario is fighting enemies, together with a third cluster corresponding to hitting the POW block, a fixed, recognizable location in the level.
At the deeper layer, the four clusters no longer correspond to this clean left/right/POW-block split: they capture visually similar configurations of Mario and the enemy near the POW block, and two of them are nearly indistinguishable.
This is reflected by the much lower \gls{cu} at layer 8b, indicating that this four-way split is not consistently recovered across retrained surrogate policies, unlike the shallower, more clearly defined clustering at layer 3.

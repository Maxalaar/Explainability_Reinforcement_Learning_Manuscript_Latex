\subsection{\MarioBrosName}

Two clusterings are examined for \MarioBrosName (Table~\ref{tab:qualitative_mario_bros_metrics}), obtained at layer 3 with $K=3$ and at layer 8b with $K=4$.

At layer 3 (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_mario_bros}), the partition is organized around where the action takes place: it separates the side of the screen on which Mario fights enemies, left (cluster A) or right (cluster C), and isolates the situations in which the POW block is present (cluster B), a fixed and recognizable element of the level.
At layer 8b (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_mario_bros_alt}), the clusters no longer follow these concepts.
They mostly reflect Mario's position in the level, a low-level concept, and some of them are hard to distinguish visually (clusters E and G).
The two clusterings reach a similar \gls{acu} ($0.50$ and $0.47$), but for opposite reasons: the one at layer 3 is highly consistent across surrogate policies (\gls{cu} of $0.84$) but stays close to the raw observation (\gls{as} of $0.66$), whereas the one at layer 8b is very abstract (\gls{as} of $0.98$) but not consistently recovered across retrained surrogates (\gls{cu} of $0.49$).

\subsection{\MarioBrosName}

Two clusterings are examined for \MarioBrosName (Table~\ref{tab:qualitative_mario_bros_metrics}), both obtained at layer 3, with $K=3$ and $K=5$.
Since the layer is the same, this comparison isolates the effect of $K$.

With $K=3$ (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_mario_bros}), the partition is organized around the location of the action.
It separates the side of the screen on which Mario fights enemies, with the left side forming cluster A and the right side forming cluster C.
It also isolates situations in which the POW block is present in cluster B, corresponding to a fixed and recognizable element of the level.

With $K=5$ (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_mario_bros_alt}), the POW block is still isolated in cluster E, but the partition no longer separates the left and right sides of the screen.
It distinguishes finer situations, mostly located on the left side of the level.
These include Mario on the top-left platform in cluster D, attacking enemies at the top left in cluster F, and hitting enemies from below in cluster H.
Cluster G corresponds to situations in which Mario moves through the level.

Increasing $K$ from $3$ to $5$ lowers \gls{cu} from $0.84$ to $0.75$ and leaves \gls{as} nearly unchanged at $0.66$ and $0.65$, resulting in a decrease in \gls{acu} from $0.50$ to $0.40$.

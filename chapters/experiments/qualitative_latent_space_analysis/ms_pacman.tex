\subsection{\MsPacManName}

Two clusterings are examined for \MsPacManName (Table~\ref{tab:qualitative_ms_pacman_metrics}), obtained at layer 6b with $K=3$ and at layer 4 with $K=5$.
Both clusterings capture the progress of the episode and the state of the game, rather than a specific spatial feature of the observation as observed in \MarioBrosName.

With $K=3$ (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_ms_pacman}), the partition separates the start of the episode in cluster B, the middle in cluster A, and the end in cluster C.

With $K=5$ (Figure~\ref{fig:qualitative_ms_pacman_alt}), the partition provides a more detailed description of the episode.
It separates the initial animation, during which the character cannot move, into cluster H and the beginning of actual play into cluster F.
It also isolates a situation specific to the game mechanics, in which Pac-Man is in booster mode and waits in front of the ghost spawn to eat the ghosts in cluster D.
Finally, it separates the end of the episode, when at most one power pellet remains, into two similar clusters, E and G.

The two clusterings have the same \gls{cu} of $0.88$, while the clustering at layer 4 has a lower \gls{as} of $0.47$ compared with $0.52$ at layer 6b.
This results in a lower \gls{acu} of $0.35$ compared with $0.40$.

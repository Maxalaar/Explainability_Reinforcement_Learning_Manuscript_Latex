\section{Latent Space Analysis}
\label{sec:experiments_latent_space_analysis}

This section analyzes their \gls{ls} using \gls{cu}, \gls{as}, and \gls{acu}.
For each environment and each layer, a random subset of 20,000 observations is embedded into the \gls{ls} of each surrogate policy, and the resulting point cloud $\textcolor{myblue}{\mathcal{E}}$ is partitioned with $k$-means clustering \cite{macqueen1967multivariate} for every number of clusters $K \in [2,20]$.

\input{figures/clustering_agent_representation/latent_space_curves_by_layer_depth.tex}

Figure~\ref{fig:latent_space_curves_by_layer_depth} reports, for each environment, \gls{cu}, \gls{as}, and \gls{acu} averaged over $K \in [2,20]$ with respect to the depth of the analyzed layer.
In every environment, \gls{cu} tends to be highest in the earliest layers and to decrease with depth, while \gls{as} follows the opposite trend, increasing with depth as the representation becomes less tied to the raw observation, the action distribution, and the network's initialization.
\gls{acu}, which combines both effects, therefore increases with depth for Mario Bros, Ms.\ Pac-Man, and Surround, for which the last analyzed layer is the most informative, whereas it peaks at an intermediate depth and decreases again for Breakout, Pong, and Space Invaders.

For each environment, the results are reported for the layer that achieves the highest average \gls{acu} across the tested values of $K$.
This layer is therefore the one for which the agreement between independently trained surrogate policies is least explained by the observation space, the action space, or the network initialization.
The following subsections then report the evolution of \gls{cu}, \gls{as}, and \gls{acu} with respect to $K$ for this layer.
The full breakdown across every analyzed layer is reported in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}.

\input{figures/clustering_agent_representation/latent_space_curves_by_metric.tex}

\subsection{Breakout}
\label{ssec:latent_space_analysis_breakout}

For Breakout, \gls{acu} is maximized at layer 6b, with $\gls{cu} = 0.59 \pm 0.06$, $\gls{as} = 0.82 \pm 0.05$, and $\gls{acu} = 0.41 \pm 0.06$ on average over $K$.
As shown in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}, \gls{cu} is highest in the earliest layers and decreases with depth, while \gls{as} follows the opposite trend, so that \gls{acu} peaks once the representation has become sufficiently abstract without yet losing cross-seed stability.

\FloatBarrier

\subsection{Mario Bros}
\label{ssec:latent_space_analysis_mario_bros}

For Mario Bros, \gls{acu} is maximized at the last analyzed layer (layer 8b), with $\gls{cu} = 0.37 \pm 0.08$, $\gls{as} = 0.94 \pm 0.02$, and $\gls{acu} = 0.31 \pm 0.08$ on average over $K$.
As shown in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}, \gls{as} increases monotonically with depth, reaching near-saturation in the last layer, while \gls{cu} decreases; \gls{acu} nonetheless keeps increasing with depth, indicating that the residual cross-seed agreement in the deepest layer is largely non-trivial.

\FloatBarrier

\subsection{Ms.\ Pac-Man}
\label{ssec:latent_space_analysis_ms_pacman}

For Ms.\ Pac-Man, \gls{acu} is maximized at the last analyzed layer (layer 8b), with $\gls{cu} = 0.58 \pm 0.08$, $\gls{as} = 0.74 \pm 0.05$, and $\gls{acu} = 0.32 \pm 0.07$ on average over $K$.
As shown in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}, the pattern mirrors Mario Bros: \gls{as} increases steadily with depth while \gls{cu} decreases, and \gls{acu} is highest in the deepest layer.

\FloatBarrier

\subsection{Pong}
\label{ssec:latent_space_analysis_pong}

For Pong, \gls{acu} is maximized at layer 4, with $\gls{cu} = 0.50 \pm 0.04$, $\gls{as} = 0.95 \pm 0.03$, and $\gls{acu} = 0.45 \pm 0.07$ on average over $K$ -- the highest \gls{acu} among the six environments.
As shown in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}, \gls{as} already saturates from layer 3 onward, and \gls{acu} decreases again in the last analyzed layer (layer 6a): unlike Mario Bros and Ms.\ Pac-Man, the most informative layer for Pong is not the deepest one.

\FloatBarrier

\subsection{Space Invaders}
\label{ssec:latent_space_analysis_space_invaders}

For Space Invaders, \gls{acu} is maximized at layer 4, with $\gls{cu} = 0.63 \pm 0.11$, $\gls{as} = 0.73 \pm 0.06$, and $\gls{acu} = 0.35 \pm 0.09$ on average over $K$.
As shown in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}, \gls{acu} decreases again toward the last analyzed layer (layer 6a), following the same non-monotonic pattern observed for Pong.

\FloatBarrier

\subsection{Surround}
\label{ssec:latent_space_analysis_surround}

For Surround, \gls{acu} is maximized at the last analyzed layer (layer 8b), with $\gls{cu} = 0.54 \pm 0.10$, $\gls{as} = 0.77 \pm 0.07$, and $\gls{acu} = 0.31 \pm 0.06$ on average over $K$.
As shown in Appendix~\ref{chap:appendix}, \gls{as} increases with depth while \gls{cu} fluctuates without a clear trend, and \gls{acu} is highest in the deepest layer.

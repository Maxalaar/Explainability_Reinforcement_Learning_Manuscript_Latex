\chapter{Experiments}
\label{chap:experiments}

\glsresetall

This chapter presents the experimental study of the \gls{car} method introduced in the previous chapter.
It focuses on the experimental validation of the method and its ability to consistently identify meaningful conceptual structures.
The remainder of this chapter is organized as follows.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_software} describes the software implementation of the \gls{car} pipeline.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_configuration} details the experimental configuration, including the environments, the reinforcement learning setup, and the training of the surrogate policies.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_results} presents and analyses the results obtained by applying \gls{car} and its metrics.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_conclusion} summarizes the findings and discusses their implications.
\end{itemize}

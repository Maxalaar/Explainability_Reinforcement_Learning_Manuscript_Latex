\chapter{Experiments}
\label{chap:experiments}

\glsresetall

This chapter presents the experimental study of the \gls{car} method introduced in the previous chapter.
It focuses on evaluating the method's ability to consistently identify meaningful conceptual structures.
The remainder of this chapter is organized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_software} describes the software implementation of the \gls{car} pipeline.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_configuration} details the experimental configuration, including the environments, the reinforcement learning setup, and the training of the surrogate policies.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_policy_training_analysis} presents the training of the initial and surrogate policies and evaluates their behavioral fidelity.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_latent_space_analysis} analyzes the latent representations obtained by applying \gls{car} and its metrics.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_qualitative_latent_space_analysis} provides a qualitative, hand-labeled analysis of the resulting clusters for each environment.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_conclusion} summarizes the findings and discusses their implications.
\end{itemize}

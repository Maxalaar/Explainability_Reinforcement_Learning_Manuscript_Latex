\chapter{Experiments}
\label{chap:experiments}

\glsresetall

This chapter reports the experimental study of the \gls{car} method introduced in Chapter~\ref{chap:clustering_agent_representation}.
While the previous chapter presented \gls{car} and its evaluation metrics from a theoretical standpoint, this chapter focuses on their concrete realization: the software used to run the experiments, the environments and training setups considered, and a detailed analysis of the results obtained.

This chapter is organized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_software_infrastructure} describes the software infrastructure implementing the \gls{car} pipeline.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_configuration} details the experimental configuration, including the environments, the reinforcement learning setup, and the training of the surrogate policies.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_results} presents and analyses the results obtained by applying \gls{car} and its metrics.
    \item Section~\ref{sec:experiments_conclusion} summarizes the findings and discusses their implications.
\end{itemize}

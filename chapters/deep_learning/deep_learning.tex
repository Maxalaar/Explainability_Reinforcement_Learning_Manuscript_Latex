\chaptercontents

\glsresetall

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of \gls{dl}, the training paradigm used to optimize deep \glspl{nn}.
The objective is to provide the necessary foundation in \gls{dl} for the ideas developed throughout the rest of this thesis.
After a brief historical overview, the chapter formalizes the objective that \gls{dl} algorithms optimize.
It then describes the structure of the \glspl{nn} that implement this objective and the learning process that shapes them.
Next, it discusses how such models are evaluated, before presenting convolutional and attention-based architectures.
Finally, it closes with a summary of the concepts introduced and a discussion of the theoretical limitations of \gls{dl}.

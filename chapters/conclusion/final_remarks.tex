\section{Final Remarks}
\label{sec:conclusion_final_remarks}

To conclude, we can step back and consider this work as a whole.
In \gls{xrl}, a common approach is to replace \glspl{nn} with simpler models that can be directly interpreted.
The approach followed in this thesis is different.
Rather than viewing the \gls{nn} as an obstacle, we propose explainability methods that build on its specificities.
From this perspective, explainability is not achieved despite the use of \glspl{nn}, but through them.
The results presented in this thesis show that this approach is feasible.
It therefore offers a way to understand our creations and to ensure that they remain aligned with our intentions.

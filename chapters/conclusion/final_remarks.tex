\section{Final Remarks}
\label{sec:conclusion_final_remarks}

To conclude, let us step back and consider this work as a whole.
We propose explainability methods that build on the specific properties of \glspl{nn}.
The goal is to uncover how these networks organize information, in order to shed light on their internal functioning.
From this perspective, explainability is not achieved despite the use of \glspl{nn}, but through them.
The results presented in this thesis show that this approach is feasible.
It thus offers a way to better understand our creations and to ensure that they remain aligned with our intentions.

\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

\glsresetall

This manuscript set out to explain the behavior of \gls{rl} agents by analyzing the internal representations of their \glspl{nn}.
This final chapter takes stock of this work.
It begins by revisiting each research question in light of the contributions presented.
It then discusses the limitations of these contributions.
Finally, it presents the directions for future research that these limitations open.

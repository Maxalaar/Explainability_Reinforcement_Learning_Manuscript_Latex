\chapter{Additional Results}
\label{chap:appendix}

This appendix reports, for each of the six Atari environments studied in Chapter~\ref{chap:experiments}, the full evolution of \gls{cu}, \gls{as}, and \gls{acu} with respect to the number of clusters $K \in [2,20]$, for every analyzed layer, together with the three baseline similarities entering the \gls{as}, namely the similarity to the observation clustering ($A^{obs}$), to the untrained-network latent clustering ($A^{untr}$), and to the clustering of the action distribution, i.e., of the policy logits before the softmax ($A^{act}$), underlying the analysis of Section~\ref{sec:experiments_clustering_quality}. All these quantities are computed with the adjusted Rand index, consistent with the rest of this manuscript.

For each environment, one table is reported per metric and per baseline, with the analyzed layers as rows and $K \in [2,20]$ as columns. In each table, a cell's background shade is proportional to its value (the closer to 1, the darker); a value in \textbf{bold} is the highest value of its column, i.e., the best layer for that number of clusters; a value that is \underline{underlined} is the highest value of its row, i.e., the best number of clusters for that layer; and a value both \textbf{\underline{bold and underlined}} is simultaneously the best of its row and of its column. The last column and the last row report, respectively, the mean $\pm$ standard deviation across $K$ for each layer, and the mean $\pm$ standard deviation across layers for each $K$, with the best mean of each in bold; the bottom-right cell reports the mean $\pm$ standard deviation over the whole table. The same convention is applied to the baseline tables, where a high value (dark cell) therefore indicates a strong alignment of the latent clustering with the baseline, i.e., a \emph{lower} contribution to the \gls{as}.
